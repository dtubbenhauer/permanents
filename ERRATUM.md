# Lemma 5B.1 — the four-packet average

In the last sentence of the proof, read the intermediate equality as

$$
[B]_{S_4}=[X_3]_{S_4}.
$$

Here brackets average simultaneous relabelings of the upper and lower packet ends. The preceding three-packet calculation gives $X_3$. Averaging over all four labels therefore places brackets on both sides. The resulting identity gives the stated conclusion $[Q]_{S_4}=X_4$.
