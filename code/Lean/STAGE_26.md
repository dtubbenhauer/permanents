# Stage 26 — The symmetric survivor bubble

For every complex orthonormal k-column frame and every survivor thickness t, the occupation columns in ambient word coordinates are linearly independent. They span the previously defined supported space. Its exact dimension is choose(k+t−1,t), and the supported cap composed with its adjoint is this scalar times the identity. The proof includes t = 0.

The declarations checked individually in this stage are

- `BlockPermanents.BalancedLast.expand_independent`
- `BlockPermanents.BalancedLast.dimension`
- `BlockPermanents.BalancedLast.bubble`
