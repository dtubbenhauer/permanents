# Stage 27 — The orthogonal complement of the survivor trace

On any supported, possibly entangled pair, the remaining t = k−r letters still lie in the symmetric power of the chosen plane. Closing the r-cut gives the original terminal trace. Its energy splits exactly as the squared norm of an explicit orthogonal complement plus the squared modulus of the full trace divided by choose(k+t−1,t). Capping the complement gives zero.
