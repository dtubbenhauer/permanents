# Stage 21 — The last thin cap on a geometric subspace

Let E have orthonormal columns spanning a marked subspace of dimension s. The last cap on that subspace is given by the entries of the orthogonal projector EE† with the spectator passing through. Its bubble is s times the identity. When the conjugated marked slices lie in the span of E, this cap closes to the unrestricted full trace. The residual projector and the completed cap give the reflected-square identity on arbitrary columns, with coefficient 1/s. A unitary change of orthonormal frame leaves the cap unchanged.

- `BlockPermanents.ThreePlane.cap_bubble`
- `BlockPermanents.ThreePlane.cap_eq_trace`
