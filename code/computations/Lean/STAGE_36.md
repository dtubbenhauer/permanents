Each positive-order cut in the spectator removal is retained. The retained family can be arbitrary, including empty. Applied to the actual signed exit D in Appendix AA this splits the fixed-mark exit energy as the three- or four-label closure plus an explicit nonnegative sum. Empty spectator lists are included.
