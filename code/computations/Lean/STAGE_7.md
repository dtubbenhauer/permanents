# Stage 7 — Naturality of the four-packet transfer

For every complex matrix A from dimension d to dimension e and every thickness k, its tensor power on the four packets intertwines Q in the two dimensions. This follows from the stronger statement for every rational linear combination of permutations of the thin slots. Neither theorem requires a Schur identity or an isometry.
