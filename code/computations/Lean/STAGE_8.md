# Stage 8 — Covariance after bending the quadratic mark

On the quadratic boundary the change of coordinates T applies A to the six ordinary slots and the complex conjugate of A to the two dual marked slots. If A is a rectangular isometry from dimension d to dimension e, the pullback of the entire bent transfer matrix in dimension e is the bent transfer in dimension d. The only hypothesis on A is A†A = I. No Schur relation enters.

The declarations checked individually in this stage are

- `BlockPermanents.FourCovariance.bentChange_apply`
- `BlockPermanents.FourCovariance.bent_covariance`
